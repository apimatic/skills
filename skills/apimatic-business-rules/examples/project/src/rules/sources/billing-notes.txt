Billing notes (from the support team)

Every request needs the API key in the Authorization header. A missing or revoked key gets 401 "invalid_api_key".

A plan can only be sold in currencies it has a price for. Subscribing to a plan with no price in the
request's currency is refused with 422 "no price for currency"; add the price first.

Cancelling a subscription is immediate.

When a renewal payment fails the subscription becomes past_due, and the payment is retried on days 3, 5 and 7.
Access should continue while past_due; the subscription.cancelled webhook is sent if all retries fail.
